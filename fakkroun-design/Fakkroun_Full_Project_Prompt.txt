======================================================================
1. PROJECT VISION
======================================================================

Fakkroun is a modern, multilingual, real-time multiplayer quiz platform designed to make learning entertaining, interactive, and accessible.

The platform combines:
- Multiplayer quiz games.
- General knowledge and educational questions.
- Green economy and environmental awareness.
- Blue economy and marine biodiversity.
- Community-generated questions.
- AI-assisted question generation.
- Educational partnerships.
- Non-intrusive advertising and sponsorship.

Fakkroun should encourage users to learn while competing with friends, family, colleagues, students, and other players.

The platform should initially prioritize:
1. Green Economy.
2. Blue Economy.
3. Environmental Protection.
4. Climate Change.
5. Renewable Energy.
6. Marine Biodiversity.
7. Sustainable Development.

It should also support general knowledge, science, technology, history, geography, culture, and sports.

The platform must be scalable, maintainable, secure, responsive, and capable of supporting a large question bank and many simultaneous multiplayer rooms.

This is not simply a quiz website. It is a complete multiplayer gaming and educational ecosystem.

- Answer choices.
- Explanation.

14. DUPLICATE-ANSWER RULE
======================================================================

Implement a configurable duplicate-answer rule.

Example for one question:
- Player A answers B.
- Player B answers B.
- Player C answers A.
- Player D answers C.

If the rule is enabled, answers B from players A and B are excluded from scoring because two players submitted the same answer. Only unique responses are considered for scoring.

The rule must be configurable:
- Enabled or disabled per game.
- Applied per question.
- Configurable minimum number of identical answers required for exclusion.
- Clearly explained to players before the game starts.

Default example: two or more identical responses are excluded. Implement the rule server-side and apply it consistently to every player. Clarify in the game results that duplicated responses were excluded.

18. ADVERTISING BILLING MODEL
======================================================================

This section is financially critical. Implement it precisely.

Fakkroun has two independent advertising consumption units:
1. Advertisement impressions.
2. Sponsored-question deliveries.

They must be tracked and billed separately.

18.1 Impression billing
An impression is counted per player, per question, only when the advertisement is successfully displayed to that individual player.

Example: one advertisement successfully displayed to 10 players during one question = 10 impressions.

If an advertisement is displayed to 10 players during 5 different questions = 50 impressions.

Do not count a server-side broadcast attempt as an impression. The system must record an auditable delivery/display event.

18.2 Duplicate impression protection
Prevent double billing caused by page refresh, browser reconnection, WebSocket reconnection, duplicate client events, retried requests, or concurrent workers.

Use a unique billing reference for every billable impression. An impression must not be billed twice for the same delivery event.

18.3 Sponsored-question billing
Sponsored questions are billed separately from impressions.

Example: one sponsored question is delivered to a room containing 10 players:
- Sponsored-question consumption = 1.
- If a related advertisement is successfully displayed to all 10 players, impression consumption = 10.

Therefore:
- One sponsored-question delivery is charged once.
- Ten impressions are charged separately.

Do not charge the sponsored-question fee per player unless the administrator explicitly configures that pricing model.

18.4 Separate balances
Maintain separate balances for impression credits and sponsored-question credits. Never mix these balances.

Campaign budget, monetary payment, purchased credits, and actual consumption must be tracked separately.

18.5 Advertising ledger
Implement an immutable and auditable financial ledger.

Each transaction must record:
- Advertiser.
- Campaign.
- Advertising format.
- Consumption type.
- Quantity.
- Unit price.
- Total amount.
- Currency.
- Timestamp.
- Room.
- Question.
- Player, where applicable.
- Unique billing reference.
- Transaction status.

Prevent negative balances and duplicate financial transactions. Use database transactions and concurrency-safe operations. Provide a reconciliation mechanism between recorded display events and billing ledger entries.
